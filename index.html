“To be a welcoming multi-ethnic community where Truth is lived, Love is shared, and every person finds belonging.”

Reaching beyond walls, meeting people where they are, and building a home for the unchurched and those seeking God.

“To embody Christ’s truth through love; building authentic community where every person can belong, believe, and become.”

Reflecting God’s heart with everyday compassion through worship and service — one life, one story, one fellowship at a time.

“People who may have given up on church, but not on God.”

We exist to love people and meet real needs through grief care, divorce care, food & clothing distribution, and everyday messages of hope.
